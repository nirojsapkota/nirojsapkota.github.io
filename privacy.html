Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to the following Android games published by Niroj Sapkota ("we", "us"):

  • Arrow Gram (com.arrowgram.game)
  • Spindle Sort (com.spindlesort.game)
  • Strobe Runner (com.stroberunner.game)

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The games do not require an account.

Game progress and settings (for example, completed levels and sound preferences) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

2. Advertising

The games show ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as impressions and clicks

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. In-app purchases

Purchases, such as removing ads, are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google handles payments under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Apart from the Google services above, we do not share data with third parties.

5. Children

The games are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security

Data used by Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use the protections that Android and Google Play provide.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will change.
